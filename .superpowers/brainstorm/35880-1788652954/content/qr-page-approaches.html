A — Polaroid Grid

QR codes in polaroid-style cards with tape, slight rotations — matches the scrapbook aesthetic. Cards in a responsive grid (3-4 across on desktop, 1-2 on mobile). Each card shows QR code, label, and category tag.

B — List Cards

Horizontal cards with QR on the left, title + description + category on the right. Stacked vertically, no rotation. Cleaner and more scannable, but less playful. Better if descriptions are important.

C — Grid + Lightbox

Polaroid grid like A, but QR codes are smaller thumbnails. Clicking a card opens a lightbox with the full-size QR, description, and a direct link. Best for mobile — users tap to get a scannable-size QR.
